About This Place

Tampines Hub is Singapore's largest integrated community and lifestyle hub. This impressive facility houses a public library, swimming complex, sports hall, and various community spaces all under one roof. It's a perfect example of Singapore's innovative approach to community building.

Facilities

Tampines Round Market

⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.3 (312 reviews)

About This Food Spot

The iconic Tampines Round Market is famous for its unique circular architecture and incredible variety of local hawker food. Built in the 1980s, this market has become a beloved gathering place for residents and a must-visit destination for food lovers exploring authentic Singaporean cuisine.

Must-Try Dishes

  • 🍲 Bak Chor Mee (Minced Pork Noodles)
  • 🐔 Hainanese Chicken Rice
  • 🍜 Laksa
  • 🥟 Wanton Mee
  • 🧊 Ice Kachang
💰 Average meal: $3-8 SGD
