Before we begin

AI should support thinking.

Why try Edcafe and Snorkl rather than simply use ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude? We want to test how teacher-configured tools can keep AI focused on the learning task.

  1. Keep the learning ring-fenced.Teachers set the task, reference material and feedback criteria, where supported, instead of starting with an unrestricted chat.
  2. Keep students doing the thinking.Students should explain, check evidence and revise. Avoid cognitive offloading: letting AI do the reasoning for them.
  3. Use AI with safeguards.MOE guidance emphasises safe, effective use after students develop basic concepts and thinking skills. Our trial follows that aim; teacher review and suitable settings still matter. MOE guidance ↗
